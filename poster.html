Trade  ·  Washington and Beijing

Half the world's output says no
to Washington's tariffs

Fourteen economies — from China and Japan to the EU — formally reject the “overcapacity” case behind a new Section 301 round, as Beijing threatens “necessary measures” and the truce clock runs to January.

The US Trade Representative proposed duties on Wednesday covering economies that together produce roughly half of global output: China at 20 per cent, Vietnam 35, India 25, Japan 15. On Thursday and Friday the objections arrived in unison, each government rejecting the finding that its factories are structurally too large.

Beijing called overcapacity a “false premise” and warned that tariff wars serve no one's interests. Tokyo said it had protested; Singapore said it was “very disappointed”. The duties are meant to rebuild levies the Supreme Court struck down in February, and would land just as the US–China truce expires on 10 January.
