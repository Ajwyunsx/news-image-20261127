# RESEARCH — 27 November 2026 (Friday)

All items below are real reporting published on 26–27 Nov 2026, and all were still being
reported on Friday 27 November. One-line facts only; sources listed per item.

## Lead — trade / tariff war
- USTR (Jamieson Greer) proposed Section 301 duties on 14 economies after a months-long probe into
  "structural excess capacity": China 20% (could double to 40%), Vietnam 35%, India 25% (17% with a
  deal), Japan 15%, Singapore up to 20%, EU capped at 15%. — AP, The Hindu, Indian Express, 25–27 Nov.
- On 26–27 Nov China, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia and the EU formally rejected the "overcapacity"
  finding. China MOFA's Guo Jiakun: "Tariff wars and trade wars serve no one's interests" and Beijing
  will take "necessary measures". Japan's chief cabinet secretary Minoru Kihara protested to Washington.
  — AP / Asahi Shimbun, 27 Nov 2026 12:10 JST; Japan Today.
- Context: duties are meant to restore levies struck down by the US Supreme Court in February 2026;
  US–China truce extended to 10 Jan 2027; ~$60bn of reciprocal tariff cuts agreed. — AP.
- The 14 economies cover roughly half of global economic output. — 10 Things Global News, 27 Nov.

## Europe — ECB succession
- European Council put forward Pablo Hernández de Cos (ex-Banco de España governor, currently BIS
  general manager) to succeed Christine Lagarde as ECB president when she leaves on 7 Feb 2027.
  Laurence Boone and Markus Brunnermeier named for the executive board. — Consilium press release,
  26 Nov 2026 23:50; France 24; WSJ.
- Eurozone inflation running at about 4%, double target, driven by the Iran-war energy shock. — AP.

## Europe — EU budget summit, Brussels
- EU leaders deadlocked over the 2028–2034 budget. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz rejected the
  latest proposal and demanded deeper cuts, ruling out new borrowing; Sweden's Ulf Kristersson wants a
  smaller seven-year budget. — AP/WORLD, 27 Nov.
- Macron called the €204bn of cuts to the Commission's original proposal a "strategic mistake" and said
  France will oppose further reductions. Merz insists all 27 states back a €45bn loan for Ukraine in 2027,
  against resistance from Slovakia, Hungary and Czechia. — Anadolu morning briefing 27 Nov; The Guardian.
- Council President António Costa floated reviving an EU digital services tax; Washington warned against it.

## Middle East — Yemen / Kuwait
- Houthi-run health ministry: an air strike on a densely populated area in south-western Yemen killed at
  least 11 people, including children (27 Nov). — AP via KYW/Audacy, 27 Nov.
- Saudi-led coalition said it destroyed 50 Houthi "legitimate military targets" in Sanaa, Al Hudaydah and

## Africa — DR Congo Ebola
- DRC Ebola outbreak: 12,021 confirmed cases and 5,904 deaths, government data released 26 Nov. Started in
  May in Ituri province; now in 8 of 26 provinces. Fastest-growing Ebola outbreak on record, caused by the
  rare Bundibugyo strain with no approved vaccine. Fighting in Ituri has blocked response teams. — UN/WHO
  via Straits Times and 10 Things, 26–27 Nov. UN peacekeepers stepped up civilian protection in Djugu.
- Also 27 Nov: 31 killed when a fuel tanker overturned and burned on a Cairo ring-road, 27 vehicles destroyed.
  — AP/WORLD, 27 Nov; driver detained.

## Asia-Pacific — space
- Rocket Lab flew its 101st Electron mission, "Night Owls Never Sleep", from Mahia, New Zealand at 13:15
  NZDT on 27 Nov, putting a StriX SAR satellite for Japan's Synspective into a 569 km orbit — its 22nd
  launch of 2026, a company annual record and the 14th for Synspective. — Rocket Lab release, 27 Nov.

## Americas — cyber
- Reuters exclusive (27 Nov): hackers breached Cloud One, the US Air Force's flagship cloud platform run
  with Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle and Google. Intrusion found during an upgrade within the past month;
  attackers reached the control plane via a software flaw. Back-office migrations paused, app updates
  blocked, some admin rights revoked; US Cyber Command called in. — Reuters, 27 Nov.

## Technology / trade — Nvidia

  loan for a Malaysian data centre. — FT/WSJ/Reuters, 26 Nov, still developing 27 Nov.
